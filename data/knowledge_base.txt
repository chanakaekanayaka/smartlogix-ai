SmartLogix Logistics Knowledge Base
=====================================
This knowledge base contains SmartLogix policies, procedures and frequently
asked questions. The Retrieval Agent searches this content to answer customer
questions about shipping, warehousing, packaging, tracking and pricing.


1. SHIPPING POLICIES
--------------------
All shipments must be booked at least 12 hours before the requested pickup time.
Standard deliveries are completed within 2 to 4 days depending on distance.
Express deliveries are completed within 1 to 2 days. Same-day delivery is
available only within the same city or district. Deliveries to the Northern and
Eastern regions such as Jaffna, Kilinochchi, Vavuniya, Batticaloa and Ampara may
take one extra day because the routes are longer.

A shipment is considered delayed if it is not delivered within one day of the
expected delivery date. Customers are notified automatically when a shipment is
delayed. If a delivery fails because no one is available to receive the item, the
item is returned to the nearest warehouse and a re-delivery is scheduled at no
extra cost for the first retry.


2. TRANSPORT AND VEHICLES
-------------------------
SmartLogix selects the vehicle based on the weight and size of the shipment.
Motorbikes carry items under 5 kg such as documents and small parcels.
Three-wheelers carry items up to 20 kg. Vans carry items up to 50 kg. Lorries
carry items up to 120 kg such as furniture. Trucks carry heavy items above 120 kg
such as large appliances and bulk furniture sets.

The cheapest route is usually the shortest road distance between the warehouse and
the destination. The fastest route may cost more because it uses express handling
and priority vehicles. Fragile and heavy items may require a larger or specially
equipped vehicle even if they are light, to ensure safe transport.


3. WAREHOUSING
--------------
SmartLogix operates ten warehouses located in Colombo, Gampaha, Kandy, Galle,
Jaffna, Kurunegala, Anuradhapura, Batticaloa, Matara and Negombo. The Colombo and
Negombo warehouses operate 24 hours a day and offer cold storage. The Kandy
warehouse also offers cold storage.

When a customer places an order, the system selects the warehouse that has the
product in stock and is closest to the destination, so that delivery time and cost
are reduced. If the nearest warehouse does not have enough available space or does
not handle the product type, the next most suitable warehouse is chosen. Fragile
goods can only be stored in warehouses that support fragile handling. Cold storage
items can only be stored in the Colombo, Kandy and Negombo warehouses.

Each warehouse has a maximum capacity measured in units. When a warehouse is close
to full, new stock is routed to another warehouse in the same region.


4. INVENTORY CONTROL
--------------------
Every product has a stock quantity and a reorder level. When the stock quantity
falls to or below the reorder level, the system flags the product for restocking
with a low stock alert. If a customer requests a product that is out of stock at
the nearest warehouse, the system checks other warehouses for availability. If the
product is completely out of stock everywhere, the customer is informed and offered
an estimated restock date. Inventory levels are updated every time an order is
confirmed so that stock information stays accurate.


5. PACKAGING GUIDELINES
-----------------------
Proper packaging protects goods during transport and reduces damage. The packaging
type is chosen automatically based on the product category and its cost is added to
the total delivery cost. Documents use padded envelopes. Electronics use anti-static
boxes with foam. Fragile items use bubble wrap with double boxing and fragile labels.
Groceries and cold items use insulated boxes. Furniture uses corner guards and moving
blankets. Large appliances use wooden crates. Books use corrugated boxes. Clothing
uses waterproof poly mailers.

Fragile items and cold storage items require the highest protection levels. Items
that require cold storage are packed with insulated materials and, where needed,
ice packs to maintain the cold chain during transport.


6. INFORMATION FLOW AND TRACKING
--------------------------------
Customers can track their shipments at any time. The system shares the current
location, the assigned warehouse, the vehicle type, the estimated delivery date and
the current status. The status values are booked, packed, in transit, delivered,
delayed and failed. When a shipment moves from one stage to the next, the status is
updated and the customer is notified. This transparent information flow lets
customers know exactly where their items are and when they will arrive.


7. PRICING
----------
The total delivery cost is made up of a base fee, a distance charge per kilometer, a
weight charge per kilogram, a packaging cost based on the item type, and a delivery
mode multiplier. Express and same-day deliveries cost more than standard and economy
deliveries. The same pricing rules are applied to all customers and all regions to
keep pricing fair and transparent. Every quote includes a breakdown so the customer
can see how the price was calculated.


8. RESPONSIBLE USE AND DATA PRIVACY
-----------------------------------
SmartLogix protects customer data. Personal details such as names, addresses and
contact numbers are used only to complete deliveries and are never sold or shared
with third parties. Customers can request to view, correct or delete their data at
any time. Every cost estimate includes an explanation of how it was calculated, so
customers understand and trust the pricing. Estimates are clearly marked as
approximate when they depend on assumptions or on data that may change.


9. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
-----------------------------
How do I get a delivery cost estimate? Enter the origin, destination and the item
you want to send, and the system returns the route, cost, delivery time and an
explanation.

Which areas do you deliver to? SmartLogix delivers across all provinces of Sri
Lanka, including the Western, Central, Southern, Northern, Eastern, North Western,
North Central, Uva and Sabaragamuwa provinces.

How is the delivery cost calculated? It is based on distance, item weight,
packaging and the delivery mode you choose. The cheapest option uses the shortest
route and standard handling; the fastest option uses express handling.

What if my item is fragile? Fragile items are packed with bubble wrap and double
boxing, shipped in a suitable vehicle, and stored only in warehouses that handle
fragile goods.

What if the item needs to stay cold? Cold storage items are packed with insulated
materials and ice packs, and are handled through the Colombo, Kandy or Negombo
warehouses which have cold storage.

What happens if my delivery is delayed? You are notified automatically, and the
system shows the updated expected delivery date. If a delivery fails, the item
returns to the nearest warehouse and a free re-delivery is scheduled.

Can I cancel or change an order? Orders can be changed or cancelled before the item
is packed. Once the item is in transit, changes are not possible.
